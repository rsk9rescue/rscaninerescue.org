Rural. Volunteer‑Driven. Mission‑Focused.

Saving dogs—and strengthening communities—across rural south Mississippi.

RS Canine Rescue is a nonprofit based in Sumrall, Mississippi, serving Lamar, Forrest, Perry, Marion, and Jefferson Davis Counties. We focus on dogs who are overlooked, abandoned, or at risk in rural areas with limited animal services.

Why we exist

Rural south Mississippi has thousands of dogs with nowhere to go—no dedicated rescue facility, limited shelter space, and few low‑cost veterinary options. RS Canine Rescue was built to change that, one dog and one community at a time.

Our mission

RS Canine Rescue exists to reduce canine overpopulation, prevent unnecessary suffering, and build community ownership of animal welfare in rural south Mississippi.

  • • Rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome dogs from rural communities.
  • • Support spay/neuter, vaccinations, and basic veterinary care.
  • • Engage adults 50+, veterans, and rural residents as core volunteers and leaders.
  • • Build a sustainable, community‑driven rescue with long‑term leadership succession.

Rural focus: We prioritize dogs in areas with no dedicated rescue facilities and limited animal control.

Volunteer‑powered: Our operations depend on trained volunteers, especially older adults and veterans.

Partnership mindset: We collaborate with shelters, veterinarians, and community organizations.

Sustainability: We plan for diversified revenue and a dedicated facility.

Future facility: rural canine care & community hub

RS Canine Rescue is planning a dedicated facility to serve as a regional hub for dog intake, care, training, and community education.

Design priorities
